Searching occurrences

Everything below talks to the live GBIF API. Back to the single-taxon components. Next: search, then open an occurrence page.

1. One search, three views

<gbif-occurrence-search> holds the filters and draws nothing. The filter panel, table and image gallery follow it and never talk to each other. Choose a country or a year range and all three change. With sync-url the filters are kept in the address (?gbif_country=…), so a search can be shared and the back button works.

2. A subset given as a predicate

A page author's subset can be a GBIF predicate, which can say more than plain filters (or, not, a geometry, a long list of datasets). Here: Puma in Argentina or Chile. The subset applies in full to everything, the panel lists only what it leaves, and the visitor's filters narrow it further.
